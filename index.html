The residence

A French Country home of 4,719 square feet, now in progress.

Five bedrooms and five and a half baths, with a two-story foyer under a 22′6″ ceiling. Brick and cast stone, three arched French doors and three roll-top dormers. The family room, kitchen and dining room open to a covered outdoor living area. The renderings and plans shown here are based on the June 3, 2026 drawings.
