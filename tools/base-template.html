The residence

A Tudor-style residence of 4,450 square feet, now in progress.

Six bedrooms, five and a half baths and two living rooms, with a two-story foyer and a chef's kitchen. The family room, kitchen and dining room open to a covered outdoor living area and the pool. The renderings and plans shown here are based on the November 2025 drawings.
